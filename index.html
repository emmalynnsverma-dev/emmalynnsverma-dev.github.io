Which agencies should an IT firm pursue?

We cleaned 25,523 FY2025 federal IT contracts in Virginia ($135B) out of a 286-column USAspending.gov extract. ANOVA showed civilian agencies' typical contracts are 9 to 55 times larger than DoD's. Chi-square showed small-business win rates vary sharply, from 89% at VA to 23% at DoD.

Excel, one-way ANOVA, chi-square
